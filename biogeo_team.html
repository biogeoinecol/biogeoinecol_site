Andrés Lira Noriega

Researcher, Investigadores por México program (CONAHCyT) · Instituto de Ecología, A.C. (INECOL) · 2014–present

Andrés studies species distributions across multiple spatial and temporal scales. He combines data from natural history collections, field observations, and laboratory studies with ecological niche modeling, geographic information systems (GIS), and remote sensing to develop a predictive and descriptive understanding of why species occur where they do.

His current research focuses on predictive models for invasive species of agricultural and forestry importance, alongside collaborative projects in conservation biology and macroecology. He particularly enjoys collaborative research, which he sees as a productive way to learn and advance science together.

Adriana Calahorra-Oliart

2025–present

Adriana received her PhD from the Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM) in 2022. Her research combines bioinformatics and evolutionary modeling to explore patterns of morphospace occupancy. She works primarily, though not exclusively, with phyllostomid bats.
